1. The Strategic Objective

SaaS marketing layouts regularly suffer from excessive visual clutter, resulting in increased visitor friction points inside pricing segments. The objective of this project was to construct a lightning-fast, interactive layout structure that dynamically changes pricing states seamlessly without causing standard page flashing or rendering lag.
