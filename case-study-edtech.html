The EduStream Dashboard

Bridging pedagogical workflows with elite interaction architecture to empower digital learning discovered flows.

Role Lead UX/UI & Frontend
Context Instructional Tech Concept
Timeline 2 Weeks (2026)
Stack Penpot, HTML5, CSS Grid

1. The Problem Space

Modern learning portals are routinely built with heavy backend architecture but deeply fragmented user interfaces. Drawing from years of real-world curriculum orchestration under the Ghana Education Service framework, I isolated a recurring issue: students and facilitators constantly lose friction-free access to core learning resources due to overcrowded data matrices.

2. The Human-Centered Concept

The core objective was to decouple complex curriculum paths into simple visual flows. Using a layout optimized for diverse viewports, the EduStream framework introduces an active tracking dashboard requiring zero training to navigate.
